小鹿学习支持 Support

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 10 日

小鹿学习是一款供家长和孩子整理单词、练习朗读、拼写及听写的英语学习工具。应用不需要账号，没有广告、社交功能或应用内购买，未集成第三方广告、分析或追踪 SDK。

1. 相机、照片与文字识别

应用仅在您主动扫描时申请相机权限。相册导入使用 Apple 系统照片选择器，仅处理您选择的图片。文字识别使用 Apple Vision 在设备上进行；应用不会把扫描图片上传给开发者或第三方。

2. 本地数据

单词本、分类、单元和发音偏好保存在设备本地。文章原文、翻译结果和听写输入用于当前练习。应用没有开发者运营的用户数据服务器。设备备份是否包含应用数据由您的 Apple 备份设置决定。

3. 朗读与翻译

朗读使用 Apple 系统语音。应用不录制声音，也不申请麦克风权限。Apple Translation 在设备上通过语言模型处理文字，系统可能需要联网下载语言资源。资源下载由 Apple 系统管理，其相关处理受 Apple 的隐私政策约束。资源未安装、下载取消或系统不支持时，翻译可能不可用；内置资料、本地单词本和手动编辑仍可使用。

4. 单词资料补全

音标、中文释义、例句和配图优先来自应用内置离线资料，其次使用设备上的已有缓存。中文释义和例句中文可由 Apple 系统翻译补充，未收录的资料可手动编辑。缓存只保存在本机。

当前版本 1.0（构建8）不包含第三方在线词典客户端或在线图片搜索，不向第三方词典发送词条、图片、文章或练习内容。旧版本保存的联网补全设置不会在此版本中启用请求。

5. 儿童与数据披露

应用不创建儿童档案，不收集姓名、联系方式、位置、设备标识符或使用分析数据，不进行追踪，也不向第三方出售或共享个人数据。建议由家长协助导入和检查学习资料。

6. 删除与联系我们

您可以在应用内编辑或删除单词、单元和分类。删除应用会移除设备上的应用本地数据；系统备份中的副本需通过 Apple 备份设置管理。如有隐私问题，请联系 stchenyubin@outlook.com。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Xiaolu Learning helps families organise vocabulary and practise pronunciation, spelling, and dictation. The app requires no account, contains no advertising or in-app purchases, and includes no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

1. Camera, Photos, and OCR

Camera access is requested only when you choose to scan. Photo import uses Apple's system photo picker and processes only selected images. OCR is performed on-device with Apple Vision. Scanned images are not uploaded to the developer or third parties.

2. Local Data

Word lists, categories, units, and pronunciation preferences are stored locally on the device. Article text, translation results, and dictation input are used for the current activity. The app has no developer-operated user-data server. Apple backup behaviour depends on your device settings.

3. Speech and Translation

Speech uses Apple system voices. The app does not record audio or request microphone access. Apple Translation processes text using on-device language models. Apple may download language resources when they are not installed; these downloads are managed by the system and governed by Apple's privacy policy. Translation may be unavailable if resources are missing, downloads are cancelled, or the system does not support it. Bundled content, local word lists, and manual editing remain available.

4. Vocabulary Completion

Phonetics, Chinese meanings, examples, and illustrations first use bundled offline content, then existing on-device cache entries. Apple Translation can supply Chinese meanings and example translations. Missing content can be edited manually. The cache remains on the device.

Version 1.0 (build 8) contains no third-party online dictionary client or online image search. It does not send vocabulary queries, images, passages, or learning content to a third-party dictionary. An online-completion preference saved by an older version cannot enable requests in this version.

5. Children and Data Disclosure

The app does not create child profiles or collect names, contact details, location, device identifiers, or usage analytics. It does not track users, sell personal data, or share personal data with third parties. Parents should assist with importing and reviewing learning material.

6. Deletion and Contact

You can edit or delete words, units, and categories in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data from the device; copies in system backups are managed through Apple backup settings. Privacy questions can be sent to stchenyubin@outlook.com.
