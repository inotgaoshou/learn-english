常见问题

扫描没有识别到完整单词：请保持纸张平整、光线充足，并在识别结果页编辑后保存。

没有音标或释义：补全会依次检查内置资料、本地缓存和 Free Dictionary API，中文可使用 Apple 系统翻译。可在首页关闭联网；查询失败时可点击“重新查询”或手动编辑。

没有声音：请检查媒体音量，并确认设备未连接到其他蓝牙音频设备。切换单词或口音后可以再次点击重听。

翻译不可用：Apple Translation 可能需要先下载英语和简体中文语言资源。请稍后重试，或直接手动编辑中文内容。

Common Questions

OCR missed part of a word: Keep the page flat and well lit, then edit the recognition result before saving.

Phonetics or a meaning are missing: Completion checks bundled data, the local cache, and Free Dictionary API in that order. Chinese text can use Apple Translation. Online completion can be disabled on the home screen; after a failure, retry or edit the fields manually.

There is no sound: Check media volume and Bluetooth audio output. Change the word or accent, then tap replay.

Translation is unavailable: Apple Translation may need to download English and Simplified Chinese resources. Try again later or edit the Chinese text manually.
