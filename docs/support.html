常见问题

KET 词库来源：以剑桥 A2 Key / Key for Schools 2025 年 8 月官方备考词表为基准，资料来源和许可可在词库页查看。这份词表不是考试所有可能词汇的穷尽清单。

错词何时移出：在两个连续的独立复习轮次中首次答对后移出待复习。原题改正不会增加通过次数。学习记录保存在本机。

扫描没有识别到完整单词：请保持纸张平整、光线充足，并在识别结果页编辑后保存。

没有音标或释义：资料补全使用内置 KET 词库和本地缓存，中文还可使用 Apple 系统翻译。未收录或有误的资料可以手动编辑。应用不使用第三方在线词典。

没有声音：请检查媒体音量，并确认设备未连接到其他蓝牙音频设备。切换单词或口音后可以再次点击重听。

翻译不可用：Apple Translation 可能需要先下载英语和简体中文语言资源。请稍后重试，或直接手动编辑中文内容。

Common Questions

KET vocabulary source: Based on the August 2025 Cambridge A2 Key / Key for Schools preparation list. Sources and licenses are available in the vocabulary screen. The official list is not an exhaustive list of every word that may appear in the exam.

When are mistakes cleared? A word leaves pending review after its first answer is correct in two consecutive independent review rounds. Correcting the same question does not add a pass. Learning records stay on your device.

OCR missed part of a word: Keep the page flat and well lit, then edit the recognition result before saving.

Phonetics or a meaning are missing: Completion uses bundled KET vocabulary and the local cache. Chinese text can also use Apple Translation. Missing or incorrect information can be edited manually. The app does not use third-party online dictionaries.

There is no sound: Check media volume and Bluetooth audio output. Change the word or accent, then tap replay.

Translation is unavailable: Apple Translation may need to download English and Simplified Chinese resources. Try again later or edit the Chinese text manually.
